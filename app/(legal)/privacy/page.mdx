# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 September 2026_

Kairo is built so that we know as little about you as possible. This page explains what data exists, where it lives, and who can see it.

## The short version

- Kairo has **no account, no telemetry and no analytics**. We do not run a server that the app talks to.
- The only services the app ever connects to are **OpenAI** (with your own key, for Pro features) and **Polar** (to check your license).
- Everything else stays on your Mac.

## Data that stays on your Mac

- **The text you select.** Kairo captures it to read it aloud or transform it. It is kept in memory for the duration of the action, and in a local history you can clear from the app.
- **Your OpenAI API key** and **your Kairo Pro license key.** Stored as files readable only by your macOS user account. They are never sent to us.
- **Your settings** (voice, speed, output language).

## Data sent to OpenAI

When you use an OpenAI voice or an AI action, Kairo sends the selected text to OpenAI's API using your key. OpenAI processes it under [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) and API data usage terms. Kairo sends nothing else: no identifiers, no usage statistics. If you never add an OpenAI key, Kairo never contacts OpenAI.

## Data sent to Polar

When you buy Kairo Pro, [Polar](https://polar.sh), our Merchant of Record, collects your email address and billing details to process the payment, issue an invoice and send you a license key. Polar acts as an independent controller for that purchase under [Polar's privacy policy](https://polar.sh/legal/privacy).

To confirm your license is active, the app sends your license key to Polar at startup, at most once every 14 days. No other data is included.

## Data we hold

We receive from Polar the list of purchases: your email address, the date, and the license key. We use it only to answer support requests and process refunds. We keep it for as long as legally required for accounting, then delete it.

When you email [contact@kairoagent.io](mailto:contact@kairoagent.io), we keep the conversation to help you and delete it once the matter is closed.

## This website

kairoagent.io uses [Umami](https://umami.is), a privacy-focused audience measurement tool, to count visits and see which pages are read. Umami sets no cookies and stores no personal data: IP addresses are not kept, and there is no identifier that could follow you across visits or sites. This is why the site shows no consent banner. Umami's own practices are described in [Umami's privacy policy](https://umami.is/privacy). Beyond that, the site is static and logs no personal data on our side.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email [contact@kairoagent.io](mailto:contact@kairoagent.io) and we will respond within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).
