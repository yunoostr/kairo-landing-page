# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 3 September 2026_

We want you to buy Kairo Pro because it earns its place on your Mac, not because you are stuck with it.

## 14 days, no questions asked

If Kairo Pro is not for you, email us within **14 days of purchase** and we will refund you in full. No form, no justification needed. This applies whether or not you have activated the license.

## How to ask

Send an email to [contact@kairoagent.io](mailto:contact@kairoagent.io) from the address you used at checkout, or reply to your Polar purchase email. Include your order number if you have it handy. We usually answer within two business days.

## How you get your money back

Refunds are issued by [Polar](https://polar.sh), our Merchant of Record, to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, the money shows up within 5 to 10 business days.

## What happens to the app

Once refunded, your license key is deactivated. Pro features (OpenAI voices and AI actions) lock the next time Kairo checks the license. The free version keeps working with macOS voices, and nothing on your Mac is deleted.

## What we cannot refund

- **OpenAI usage.** Kairo runs on your own OpenAI API key. Anything OpenAI billed you is between you and OpenAI.
- **Requests after 14 days.** Write to us anyway if something went wrong; we read every message, but a refund is not guaranteed.
- **Repeated purchase and refund cycles.** We reserve the right to decline refunds that look like abuse.

## Your legal rights

This policy adds to, and does not replace, the rights you have as a consumer under the law of your country, including the EU right of withdrawal.
